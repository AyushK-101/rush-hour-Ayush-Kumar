# Presentation

Put your deck here as **PDF** (preferred) or PowerPoint, for example `deck.pdf`. The self-check looks for a `.pdf`, `.pptx`, `.key`, or `.odp` file in this folder.

## Format: about 5 slides, 5 minutes

1. **The problem (45 s).** Who loses time, where, and what is at stake for the city.
2. **Our solution (60 s).** How the system works, with one picture of a route comparison.
3. **The evidence (75 s).** Held-out results, route results against the baseline, and what the numbers do not show.
4. **Making it real (75 s).** Service model, funding and operator payments, pilot cost, risks, rollout and KPIs.
5. **The ask (45 s).** The decision you want from the city, and the next step.

## Tips

* Lead with the answer on every slide. State every assumption and label illustrative figures as such.
* Pull numbers from `results/` and `docs/`, so the deck and the repository agree.
* Rehearse the two questions you hope nobody asks.

Speaker notes or a script (optional): put them in `presentation/pitch_notes.md`.
